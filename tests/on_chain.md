## What did not go as written

- **The product page was read as unclear, not as an overlap.** The run was written expecting `110` for b10, a
  550-word product page. The two readings in the leader's block did not name the same tiers, and the value the
  nodes stored was `?`, agreed 3 to 2: nothing booked, no bond moved, the payment back, the brief spent on C1. The
  run was stopped when the next check, which needed a frozen card, failed. The expectation for b10 was changed to accept either
  outcome, and three steps were added before the overlap was tried again in other words: b11 (the diploma), b12
  (the product page sent again, refused as spent) and b13 (a general text of 550 words, a size inside both
  tiers' ranges). The runner then continued from its saved state and sent nothing twice; the
  table above is the one sequence on chain.
- **b13 took two rounds.** Its first round was carried by no majority, 2 agree and 3 disagree, and stored nothing.
  Studio rotated the leader, and the second round stored `110` with 3 agree and 2 idle. It is one transaction; it
  took 32 minutes from sending to finality. The overlap is the reading that moves the maker's money, and it is the
  one that was hardest to agree on.
- **C2's reading of the same brief** was carried 3 agree, 1 disagree, 1 idle.
